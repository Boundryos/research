# About

**Ben Mueller.** Principal of an Australian accounting practice specialising in family day care tax, and founder of Boundry.

I come to AI governance from regulated practice. In accounting and tax, every figure has to be traceable and defensible, and "trust me" is never an acceptable answer to "how did you get that number?" When I began using AI in my own practice, I needed the same standard for AI-assisted work: a way to let AI help without losing the ability to show exactly what was done, under which rule, and on whose authority.

Boundry grew out of that need. I hold a Master of Applied Taxation and a Bachelor of Business, and I am a registered tax agent. Boundry's live uses so far are of its record layer, in a bookkeeping application and a financial reconciliation application. Its governing core has been demonstrated in our own development programme. I developed it with AI-assisted engineering tools under a documented, governed process, in which proposed changes were checked against the substrate's own rules and recorded. That is described in [Essay 02](essays/02-refusal-driven-development.md).

I'm interested in hearing from researchers working on agent guardrails, AI control, verifiable computation and rules as code, particularly anyone who would like to examine, test or challenge the claims here.
